From responsible sourcing to finished product

How we process, from field to dispatch

Once crops are responsibly sourced, they move through a controlled process designed to protect quality at every stage. At our processing facility, incoming crops are received, inspected, sorted, cleaned, and processed under controlled conditions. Products are dried or frozen, packaged, batch-coded, and recorded to support quality control and traceability from farmer delivery through final dispatch.

01

Farm sourcing

Products are sourced from trusted local farming communities.

02

Collection and transport

Harvested products are carefully collected and transported under controlled conditions.

03

Processing facility

Crops are inspected, sorted, cleaned, and processed under controlled conditions, then dried or flash-frozen and batch-coded for traceability.

04

Packaging and export

Final products are packaged securely and prepared for international markets.
